## Overview

Don’t buy a $9K RTX 5090.... instead.

A $9K graphics processing unit (GPU) like the RTX 5090 is not a necessary purchase for most users. In fact, it might even be more expensive than buying a comparable gaming PC with a higher-end GPU. The increased price of the RTX 5090 is primarily due to its high power consumption and large thermal design power (TPP). This makes it less suitable for use in portable devices or those with limited power sources.

In a scenario where a user is using a gaming laptop, the RTX 5090 would be more of a novelty than a practical choice. The increased power consumption would require a more powerful power supply unit (PSU), which would add weight and cost to the laptop. Additionally, the high power consumption would also increase the risk of overheating, which could lead to reduced performance and increased repair costs. For a user who only plays games on the laptop, the benefits of a more powerful GPU might not outweigh the drawbacks.

The RTX 5090's high price and limited practical use cases make it an unnecessary purchase for most users. Instead, consider investing in a more affordable and practical graphics card that can provide comparable performance for a lower price. A more budget-friendly option might include the RTX 3060 or 3070, which offer similar performance at a fraction of the cost.
